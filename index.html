About TouristQR

**TouristQR** is a next-generation smart tourism discovery network. By placing scannable QR codes near cultural, architectural, and historical landmarks, we connect physical explorers instantly with rich digital archives, sponsor stories, and interactive navigation tools right on their smartphones.
